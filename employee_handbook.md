# Employee Handbook

**Document Owner:** Human Resources
**Last Updated:** March 2026
**Applies To:** All employees

## 1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch
break. Flexible start times between 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM are permitted with manager agreement,
provided the employee completes 8 working hours per day.

## 2. Leave Policy
- **Paid Time Off (PTO):** 24 days per calendar year, accrued monthly, for full-time employees.
- **Sick Leave:** 12 days per calendar year, non-carry-forward.
- **Public Holidays:** As per the company's published holiday calendar, typically 10-12 days
  per year depending on location.
- Unused PTO up to 10 days may be carried forward to the next calendar year; any excess lapses
  on December 31.
- Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance for
  planned leave; sick leave should be reported to the manager as early as possible on the day of
  absence.

## 3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote
arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are evaluated on a role-by-role basis.

## 4. Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect, and avoid conflicts
of interest. Any conflict of interest, including outside employment or financial interest in a
vendor or competitor, must be disclosed to HR.

## 5. Reimbursement of Business Expenses
Business expense reimbursement is governed by the separate Expense Policy and Travel Policy.
Employees should refer to those documents for expense limits, receipt requirements, and the
approval workflow.

## 6. Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October. Compensation
adjustments, where applicable, are typically effective from April 1 following the annual review
cycle.

## 7. Onboarding and Probation
New employees serve a probation period of 3 months from their date of joining, during which
either party may terminate employment with 15 days' notice. Confirmation of employment is
communicated in writing at the end of the probation period, subject to satisfactory performance.

## 8. Resignation and Notice Period
- Employees below Manager level: 30 days' notice period.
- Employees at Manager level and above: 60 days' notice period.
- The company may, at its discretion, allow buyout of the notice period or a shorter transition,
  subject to manager and HR approval.

## 9. Workplace Harassment and Grievance Redressal
The company maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment of any kind. Employees can
report concerns confidentially to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline listed on the
intranet. All complaints are investigated by the Internal Committee within statutory timelines.

## 10. Equipment and IT Assets
Laptops, mobile devices, and other equipment issued to employees remain company property and
must be returned upon separation. Loss or damage due to negligence may be recovered from the
employee's final settlement.

## 11. Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential business information, client data, or proprietary
technical information, both during employment and after separation, in line with the
confidentiality agreement signed at the time of joining.
