# Travel Policy

**Document Owner:** Finance Department & Admin
**Last Updated:** February 2026
**Applies To:** All employees traveling on company business

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the booking, class of travel, and reimbursement rules for all
company-related domestic and international travel.

## 2. Travel Approval
- All domestic travel must be approved by the employee's manager at least 3 business days in
  advance, except for urgent client-facing travel.
- All international travel must be approved by the department head and HR at least 7 business
  days in advance, and requires a valid business visa where applicable.

## 3. Booking Process
- All flights, trains, and hotels must be booked through the company's designated travel desk
  or approved travel portal. Direct bookings outside the portal are reimbursed only in
  exceptional cases with prior approval and will be reimbursed at the lower of actual cost or
  the portal's equivalent fare.

## 4. Class of Travel
- **Air travel (domestic):** Economy class for all employees.
- **Air travel (international), flight duration under 6 hours:** Economy class.
- **Air travel (international), flight duration 6 hours or more:** Premium economy for
  employees at Manager level and above; economy for all others.
- **Rail travel:** AC 2-tier or equivalent for domestic journeys over 4 hours; AC chair car or
  equivalent for shorter journeys.
- Business class travel requires VP-level approval regardless of flight duration.

## 5. Lodging While Traveling
- Employees should book hotels through the approved travel portal, within the lodging limits
  defined in the Expense Policy.
- Extended stays beyond the business need (e.g., personal vacation days attached to a business
  trip) are permitted, but the company will only cover costs for the business portion of the
  trip; personal-day costs must be paid by the employee.

## 6. Ground Transportation
- Airport transfers via company-approved ride-share or taxi services are reimbursable.
- Rental cars require pre-approval from the manager and are reimbursable only when public
  transport or ride-share is impractical (e.g., remote client sites).
- Mileage for personal vehicle use on business travel is reimbursed at the rate published
  annually by Finance (see the Finance Policy for the current per-kilometer rate).

## 7. Per Diem
- Employees on multi-day domestic trips may claim a per diem instead of itemized meal receipts,
  at INR 1,200 per day.
- Per diem is not available for international trips; itemized meal receipts under the Expense
  Policy limits apply instead.
- Per diem and itemized meal reimbursement cannot both be claimed for the same day.

## 8. Travel Insurance
The company provides travel insurance for all approved business trips. Employees must use the
company-provided insurance and should not purchase separate travel insurance for reimbursement.

## 9. Combining Business and Personal Travel
Employees may combine personal travel with a business trip subject to manager approval. The
company will reimburse only the airfare cost equivalent to what a direct business-only itinerary
would have cost, and none of the incremental lodging or transportation costs for personal days.

## 10. Safety and Emergency Protocol
- Employees traveling internationally must register their itinerary with the Admin team before
  departure.
- In case of a travel emergency, employees should contact the 24/7 Travel Assistance hotline
  listed on the company intranet.

## 11. Non-Compliance
Travel bookings made outside this policy without prior approval may not be reimbursed, and
repeated non-compliance may be escalated to HR.
