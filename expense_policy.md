# Expense Policy

**Document Owner:** Finance Department
**Last Updated:** January 2026
**Applies To:** All full-time and part-time employees

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how employees may incur, submit, and get reimbursed for business-related
expenses. It applies to all expenses charged to the company, whether paid personally by an
employee or via a corporate card.

## 2. General Principles
Employees are expected to act as prudent, cost-conscious agents of the company. Expenses must
be reasonable, necessary for business purposes, and properly documented. The company will not
reimburse expenses that are personal in nature, lavish, or that violate this policy.

## 3. Reimbursable Expense Categories
The following categories are reimbursable when supported by valid receipts:
- Business travel (flights, trains, taxis, ride-share, mileage)
- Lodging during business travel
- Client and business meals
- Internet and phone charges incurred for business purposes while traveling
- Conference and training registration fees
- Office supplies not available through the standard procurement system

## 4. Expense Limits
- Meals: up to INR 1,500 per person per day for domestic travel and USD 60 per person per day
  for international travel.
- Lodging: up to INR 8,000 per night for domestic travel (metro cities) and USD 200 per night
  for international travel, unless a conference venue requires a higher rate.
- Ground transportation: actual cost is reimbursable; employees should use the lowest reasonable
  fare class (e.g., economy taxi/ride-share, not premium tiers, unless no other option exists).
- Any single expense item above INR 10,000 requires prior written approval from a manager
  before it is incurred, except in genuine emergencies.

## 5. Receipts and Documentation
- Original itemized receipts are required for any expense over INR 500.
- Credit card statements alone are not sufficient documentation; an itemized receipt must
  accompany every claim.
- For expenses without a receipt (e.g., small tips), employees may submit a signed
  self-certification for amounts under INR 500, capped at INR 2,000 per month.

## 6. Submission Process
1. Expenses must be submitted through the Expense Management System (EMS) within 30 calendar
   days of being incurred.
2. Each expense report must include: date, business purpose, attendees (for meals), category,
   and the original receipt.
3. Expense reports submitted more than 60 days after the expense was incurred will not be
   reimbursed except with CFO approval.

## 7. Approval Workflow
- Expenses under INR 25,000 require approval from the employee's direct manager.
- Expenses between INR 25,000 and INR 1,00,000 require approval from the department head.
- Expenses above INR 1,00,000 require approval from the Finance Controller in addition to the
  department head.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Items
The following are never reimbursable:
- Alcoholic beverages, except at company-sanctioned client entertainment events with prior
  approval.
- Traffic or parking fines, and any legal penalties.
- Personal entertainment (movies, spa services, mini-bar charges).
- Loss or theft of personal property while traveling.
- Spousal or family travel costs, unless explicitly approved by the CEO for a specific event.

## 9. Corporate Credit Cards
Employees issued a corporate credit card must reconcile all charges in the EMS on a monthly
basis. Misuse of a corporate card for personal expenses may result in disciplinary action,
including termination, and the employee will be required to repay the amount within 15 days.

## 10. Currency and Exchange Rates
For international expenses, reimbursement is calculated using the exchange rate on the date the
expense was incurred, as published by the company's designated bank. Employees should retain
proof of currency conversion where a card statement does not clearly show the INR equivalent.

## 11. Policy Exceptions
Any exception to this policy must be requested in writing to the Finance Controller and
approved before the expense is incurred. Retroactive exceptions are granted only in
extraordinary circumstances.
