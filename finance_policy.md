# Finance Policy

**Document Owner:** Finance Department
**Last Updated:** January 2026
**Applies To:** All employees and department budget owners

## 1. Purpose
This policy covers financial controls, budget ownership, payment terms, and mileage/per-kilometer
rates that support the Expense and Travel policies.

## 2. Budget Ownership
Each department head owns their department's annual budget and is accountable for spend against
it. Any spend that would cause a department to exceed its quarterly budget by more than 10%
requires CFO sign-off before commitment.

## 3. Mileage Reimbursement Rate
The per-kilometer mileage rate for personal vehicle use on approved business travel is reviewed
annually. The current rate, effective January 2026, is INR 12 per kilometer, inclusive of fuel
and wear-and-tear. This rate is used for calculating mileage claims referenced in the Travel
Policy.

## 4. Payment Terms for Reimbursements
- Approved expense reimbursements are processed and paid out within 10 business days of final
  approval in the Expense Management System.
- Reimbursements are paid via direct deposit to the employee's registered salary account only;
  the company does not process reimbursements via cash or third-party accounts.

## 5. Vendor Payments
- All vendor invoices must be routed through Accounts Payable and matched against a valid
  purchase order (PO) before payment.
- Standard vendor payment terms are net-30 from invoice receipt unless a different term is
  specified in the vendor contract.

## 6. Procurement Thresholds
- Purchases under INR 50,000: department head approval only.
- Purchases between INR 50,000 and INR 5,00,000: department head plus Procurement review.
- Purchases above INR 5,00,000: require a competitive quote process (minimum 3 vendor quotes)
  and CFO approval.

## 7. Financial Year and Audit
- The company's financial year runs from April 1 to March 31.
- All expense and vendor records must be retained for 7 years to comply with statutory audit
  requirements.
- Internal audit may sample expense reports quarterly; employees must retain original receipts
  for at least 1 year even after reimbursement.

## 8. Currency Conversion Reference Rate
For any multi-currency reimbursement or vendor payment, Finance uses the daily reference rate
published by the company's primary banking partner, captured at the time the transaction is
recorded in the accounting system.

## 9. Delegation of Financial Authority
Approval authority under this policy and the Expense/Travel policies may be temporarily
delegated in writing (e.g., during a manager's leave) but must be logged with HR and Finance,
and cannot be delegated more than one level down without CFO approval.

## 10. Fraud and Misrepresentation
Any deliberate misrepresentation of an expense, invoice, or financial record is treated as
financial misconduct and will be referred to HR and, where applicable, legal authorities,
regardless of the amount involved.
